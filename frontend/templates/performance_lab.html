How these numbers are calculated

Based on statuses and dates saved in your account. Drafts are excluded from sent totals. An outcome means a recorded reply, interview, acceptance or rejection; it does not mean success.

CV groups are descriptive. Different jobs, timing and sample sizes can affect outcomes; these results do not prove a CV caused a response. Applications without an owned saved version remain unidentified.
