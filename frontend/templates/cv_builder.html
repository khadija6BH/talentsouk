Use an Overleaf template

Choose a LaTeX CV template, add TalentSouk placeholders where your details belong, then download its source ZIP. Save your CV here before importing the template.

For example, replace the sample name with [[CV:FULL_NAME]]. Other fields include [[CV:JOB_TITLE]], [[CV:EMAIL]], [[CV:SUMMARY]], [[CV:EXPERIENCE]], [[CV:EDUCATION]], [[CV:SKILLS]], [[CV:PROJECTS]] and [[CV:LANGUAGES]].

Upload the filled ZIP as a new Overleaf project to preview and download your PDF.
